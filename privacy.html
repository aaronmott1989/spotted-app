Information we collect

Email — your choices

Opting in to emails is optional. Every marketing email we send includes an unsubscribe option and our mailing address, as required by the CAN-SPAM Act. To stop receiving emails, use the unsubscribe link, reply "unsubscribe," or email support@spotted.coach. We honor opt-outs promptly.

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers who help us operate, and only as needed:

  • Advertising and analytics platforms (such as Meta) to measure and improve our ads.
  • When required by law, or to protect our rights, safety, or property.

Cookies, pixels, and your choices

We use cookies and pixels (including the Meta pixel) to run and measure our advertising. You can control cookies through your browser settings. We honor recognized opt-out preference signals, including Global Privacy Control (GPC).
